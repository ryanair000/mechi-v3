## Registration Requirements

1. Create or sign into Mechi account
2. Select your game(s)
3. Enter exact in-game username (must match during tournament)
4. Provide WhatsApp number
5. Provide Instagram username
6. Provide YouTube name
7. Confirm 8:00 PM availability
8. Pay entry fee (M-Pesa)
9. Join game WhatsApp group

### Reward Eligibility
Players must:
- Follow @playmechi on Instagram
- Subscribe to PlayMechi on YouTube

Players who don't complete both can still play but are NOT eligible for prizes.

## Tournament Rules

### General Rules
1. Use the SAME username you registered with
2. Join rooms/matches on time
3. Late players may be disqualified
4. No cheating, hacking, teaming, scripts, emulator abuse
5. No toxic language, insults, threats, abuse
6. Results verified by screenshots or admin records
7. Admin decisions are final

### PUBG Mobile / CODM Rules
- Solo battle royale format
- 3 matches total
- PUBG: 1 kill = 1 point
- CODM: 1 kill = 3 points + placement points
- Top fraggers win

### eFootball Rules
- 1v1 knockout bracket
- Single-leg matches
- Draws settled in extra time or penalties
- Round of 16 to final

## Common Questions

### Registration
**Q: How do I register?**
A: Go to mechi.club/weekendcup/register, pick your game, fill the form, and pay.

**Q: Can I register for multiple games?**
A: Yes, register and pay separately for each game.

**Q: I registered but didn't pay. Is my slot saved?**
A: No. Slots are confirmed only after payment. Pending registrations may be dropped.

**Q: Can I change my username after registering?**
A: Contact support with your new username before the tournament.

### Payment
**Q: How do I pay?**
A: M-Pesa to the Paybill/Till shown after registration.

**Q: My payment isn't showing. What do I do?**
A: Wait 30 minutes. If still not showing, contact support with M-Pesa screenshot.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refunds are not automatic. Contact support for special cases.

### Tournament
**Q: What time do matches start?**
A: PUBG/CODM at 8:00 PM EAT, eFootball at 7:30 PM EAT.

**Q: How do I join the match?**
A: Check in on the dashboard, join WhatsApp group, wait for room details.

**Q: What if I'm late?**
A: Late players may be disqualified. Be ready 15 minutes early.

**Q: How are winners decided?**
A: PUBG/CODM by total kills across 3 matches. eFootball by bracket progression.

### Prizes
**Q: When do winners get paid?**
A: Within 48 hours after tournament ends, via M-Pesa.

**Q: Do I need Instagram/YouTube to win?**
A: Yes. You must follow @playmechi on Instagram AND subscribe on YouTube to be eligible for prizes.
