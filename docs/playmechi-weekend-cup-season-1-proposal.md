# PlayMechi Weekend Cup Season 1 Proposal

## 1. Event Overview

**Event name:** PlayMechi Weekend Cup Season 1  
**Organizer:** Mechi.club / PlayMechi  
**Format:** Online weekend gaming tournament  
**Games:** PUBG Mobile, Call of Duty Mobile, and eFootball  
**Proposed dates:** Friday 15 May 2026 to Sunday 17 May 2026  
**Prize pool:** KSh 7,500  
**Entry pricing:** Early Bird KSh 50, Regular KSh 75, Late Entry KSh 100  
**Streaming:** PUBG, CODM, and eFootball matches to be streamed/recorded on PlayMechi channels  

PlayMechi Weekend Cup Season 1 is planned as a controlled paid pilot tournament designed to test player willingness to pay, tournament operations, streaming, moderation, payment confirmation, check-in, and result handling before scaling into future paid tournament weekends.

## 2. Event Purpose

The purpose of this tournament is to establish PlayMechi as a trusted online gaming competition platform in Kenya by running a structured, affordable, and visible paid tournament across three popular games.

The key goals are:

1. Test paid registrations using tiered entry pricing.
2. Build trust with a public prize pool and streamed matches.
3. Validate tournament operations for PUBG Mobile, CODM, and eFootball.
4. Test payment tracking, player check-in, match-day communication, moderation, and payout flow.
5. Use Weekend Cup Season 1 as proof for larger future events.

| Tier | Price | Recommended limit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Early Bird | KSh 50 | First 12 paid players only |
| Regular Entry | KSh 75 | Main registration tier |
| Late Entry | KSh 100 | Final slots and matchday entry |

### Public pricing wording

**Entry from KSh 50**  
Early Bird KSh 50 • Regular KSh 75 • Late Entry KSh 100  
Early Bird slots are limited and confirmed only after payment.

The Early Bird price must apply to paid players only, not players who merely fill in the form. This prevents unpaid users from holding cheap slots.

## 11. Staff and Partner Roles

### Moderators

Moderators should be paid after their tournament-day responsibilities are completed.

Expected duties:

1. Confirm player check-in.
2. Share match/lobby details.
3. Monitor rules and player conduct.
4. Record and verify results.
5. Handle disputes.
6. Submit final winners.
7. Provide a short match-day report.

### Streamers

Each streamer should deliver:

1. Start stream before match begins.
2. Include PlayMechi branding in title or overlay where possible.
3. Keep stream recording available after the event.
4. Mention the tournament name and prize pool.
5. Share final stream link to the organizer.

### Ranxxy / CODM KE support

Ranxxy should not be treated only as a name placement. His value should be tied to deliverables:

1. Promote the CODM tournament.
2. Review or approve CODM rules.
3. Be available during CODM match time.
4. Help with CODM disputes if needed.
5. Repost or acknowledge CODM winners.

## 12. Payment Confirmation Flow

Weekend 1 can use manual payment verification if automatic payment integration is not ready.

Recommended flow:

1. Player registers for a game.
2. Player pays the required tier amount.
3. Player sends M-Pesa transaction code or payment proof.
4. Admin verifies payment.
5. Player is marked as paid.
6. Only paid players can check in.
7. Only checked-in players receive room/match access.

### Payment statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending payment | Player registered but has not paid |
| Paid | Payment confirmed and slot secured |
| Checked in | Player is ready for match day |
| No-show | Player failed to appear after check-in requirements |
| Disqualified | Player removed due to rule violation |
| Winner paid | Prize payout completed |

## 13. Refund Policy

Recommended public refund rules:

1. No refund after check-in closes.
2. No refund for no-show players.
3. Refund only if Mechi cancels the tournament.
4. Wrong payment amount must be topped up before a slot is confirmed.
5. A slot is only secure after payment is confirmed.
6. Admin decisions after dispute review are final.

## 14. Dispute Policy

1. Disputes must be submitted within 20 minutes after the match or incident.
2. Evidence is required where applicable.
3. False reports may lead to disqualification.
4. Moderator/admin decision is final after review.
5. Payouts should only be released after dispute windows close.

## 18. Risk Review

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Too many players take KSh 50 slots | Revenue drops | Limit Early Bird to first 12 paid players only |
| Players register but do not pay | Slots get blocked | Count only paid players as confirmed |
| Low turnout | Event may need top-up | Keep KSh 2,000–3,000 buffer |
| Disputes delay payouts | Trust issues | Use clear dispute window and evidence rules |
| Streamer fails to show | Lower visibility | Confirm streamer early and pay after delivery |
| Moderator misses duties | Poor match control | Assign clear duties and pay after results |
| Website details mismatch posters | Confusion | Update website before public promotion |

## 19. Final Recommendation

Proceed with **PlayMechi Weekend Cup Season 1** as a break-even paid pilot.

Use the tiered pricing model:

- Early Bird: KSh 50, first 12 paid slots only
- Regular Entry: KSh 75
- Late Entry: KSh 100

Keep the KSh 7,500 prize pool because it makes the event more attractive, but strictly control costs and payment confirmation. The event should focus on trust, visibility, and learning, not short-term profit.

If Weekend 1 succeeds, the next tournament can move to a higher base registration fee such as KSh 100, with better margins and a larger prize pool.
