PlayMechi helps players get through tournament night without chasing links or room codes.

Use the app to register for events, finish payment, check in, see room details when they are released, and submit your result proof after a match.

What you can do:
- Create your player profile and save your game IDs
- Register for live PlayMechi tournaments
- Pay entry fees through Paystack when required
- See your entry, payment, and check-in status
- Check in before your match
- Open room details, fixtures, standings, and match updates
- Upload a screenshot for result review
- Read official updates and use support when you need help
- Turn on alerts for room releases, check-in reminders, and proof reviews

PlayMechi is for mobile players who want the basics to be clear: what is open, what is due next, and where to go when the match starts.

Payments may open in Paystack. Tournament rooms, rewards, fees, fixtures, standings, and alerts depend on the active event and admin review.
