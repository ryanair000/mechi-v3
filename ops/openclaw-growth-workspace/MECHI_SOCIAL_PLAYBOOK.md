# Mechi Social Playbook

Use this file when `growth` or `socio` needs to write, format, or publish Mechi social content.

## Brand anchor

- Mechi is a Kenyan gaming platform for matchmaking, tournaments, rewards, subscriptions, support, and community play.
- PlayMechi is the public social brand and handle for tournament, hype, stream, clip, and community-facing posts.
- ChezaHub is the commerce/store social brand for passes, bundles, subscriptions, top-ups, and direct order CTAs.
- Public links:
  - main site: `https://mechi.club`
  - PlayMechi page: `https://mechi.club/playmechi`
  - registration link when relevant: use `https://mechi.club/weekendcup` for Weekend Cup or `https://mechi.club/tournaments` for open tournaments; PlayMechi Launch registration is closed
- Public handle: `@playmechi`

## Tone

- Energetic, sharp, and current.
- Confident without sounding fake or overhyped.
- Clear enough for Kenyan gamers who want fast context.
- Avoid corporate filler, stiff ad copy, or generic "join us now" spam.

## Non-negotiables

- Use the Boss's caption when provided. Only clean grammar, spacing, and clarity lightly.
- Do not invent tournament dates, prize pools, winners, sponsor claims, or registration deadlines.
- If a post needs a factual detail that is missing, either keep the caption evergreen or ask one short clarification.
- Do not add links unless they help the exact post goal.
- Keep hashtags tight and intentional. Do not dump a long hashtag block.

## Default caption structure

When the Boss does not provide copy, build the first draft with this order:

1. Hook
2. What is happening
3. Why it matters or what viewers should do
4. Short CTA
5. Tight hashtag finish

Example shape:

```text
Your opener here.

What the audience is seeing or why this matters.

CTA here.

#PlayMechi #MechiClub
```

## Hashtag rules

- Default base: `#PlayMechi #MechiClub`
- Add at most 1-2 topic-specific tags when they truly fit:
  - `#CODM`
  - `#PUBGMobile`
  - `#eFootball`
  - `#GamingKE`
  - `#EsportsKE`
- Do not exceed 4 hashtags unless the Boss explicitly asks for SEO-heavy copy.

## CTA rules

- Registration push: use `https://mechi.club/weekendcup` for Weekend Cup or `https://mechi.club/tournaments` for open tournaments
- General discovery or recap: use `https://mechi.club/playmechi`
- Clips, highlights, and community flex posts do not need a link every time.

## Platform shaping

### Instagram

- Strong hook and clean spacing.
- 1 short CTA is enough.
- 2-4 hashtags max unless the Boss asks otherwise.
- Caption can be slightly longer when the post is a registration, countdown, or recap.
- Explicit `socio post chezahub` or `socio post playmechi` commands should cross-post to Facebook for the same brand, not Instagram only.

#### Approved promo offer format

Use this structure for game subscription, pass, bundle, and limited-time offer posters unless the Boss provides different final copy:

```text
PRODUCT NAME is available now.

Get DURATION for only PRICE

What’s included:
• BENEFIT 1
• BENEFIT 2
• BENEFIT 3

Offer ends DATE.

Order now at DOMAIN
DM us to secure yours.

#PRIMARYTAG #SECONDARYTAG #BRANDTAG #TOPIC1 #TOPIC2 #TOPIC3 #TOPIC4 #TOPIC5
```

Formatting rules for this approved pattern:

- Keep the first line as the direct product hook.
- Keep one blank line between sections.
- Use `What’s included:` followed by `•` bullets for benefits.
- Put the end date on its own short line.
- Keep the CTA split across two lines:
  - `Order now at DOMAIN`
  - `DM us to secure yours.`
- Keep hashtags as one tight final block.

Approved saved example for the current Fortnite Crew offer:

```text
FORTNITE CREW is available now.

Order now at chezahub.co.ke
DM us to secure yours.

#FortniteCrew #Fortnite #ChezaHub #GamingDeals #KenyaGaming #BattlePass #Vbucks #FortniteKE
```

### Facebook

- Slightly more explanatory than Instagram.
- Keep the first two lines strong because preview collapse matters.
- One clear CTA is better than multiple asks.
- When the Boss names `chezahub` or `playmechi`, Facebook should follow the same brand target as Instagram.

### X

- Keep it concise and punchy.
- One idea per post.
- Prefer 0-2 hashtags.
- If a link is present, cut fluff before trimming the CTA.

### TikTok

- TikTok is paused for live SMM execution until the Boss explicitly re-enables it.
- Video-first. Use energetic captions, but do not over-explain what the viewer can already see.
- Keep captions shorter than Instagram unless the post is a registration push.
- Use 2-4 hashtags max.
- Do not use `https://mechi.club/playmechi/register` for active registration pushes; PlayMechi Launch registration is closed.
- TikTok API posting requires a valid Content Posting API token. If the app is not approved for public posting, use `SELF_ONLY` or hand off to native/manual posting.
- `tiktok-draft` sends the video to the creator's TikTok inbox for manual app approval. Tell the Boss to open TikTok, tap the inbox notification, add sound/effects, paste or adjust the caption, and post.
- If TikTok draft upload fails with a scope error, the PlayMechi TikTok token needs `video.upload`. If direct TikTok post fails with review/privacy errors, use `tiktok-draft` or keep direct posting on `SELF_ONLY` until TikTok review passes.

### Discord

- More direct and community-like.
- Short opener, then the actual action.
- Avoid polished ad-speak. Sound like an informed community drop.

## Content pattern guide

### Registration or announcement

- Lead with the game, event, or drop.
- Mention the action clearly.
- Use the registration link only when the post is actually about joining.

### Countdown or reminder

- Lead with urgency.
- Keep it clean.
- Mention the next step, not the entire tournament history.

### Winner or results

- Lead with the winner or the moment.
- Keep the tone celebratory.
- Do not mention prize details unless they were supplied or verified for that specific post.

### Clip, photo, or hype post

- React to what the audience can see.
- Keep the caption visual and emotional first.
- Do not force a link if the content already stands alone.

### Brand or platform post

- Focus on what Mechi enables:
  - better matchups
  - tournament runs
  - gamer community
  - reward momentum

## Social execution guardrails

- A photo or video dropped by the Boss inside the private `OPS -> SMM` Telegram room counts as approval to publish on Instagram unless the Boss says otherwise.
- Cross-posting to Facebook, X, or Discord needs the Boss to name those targets explicitly, for example:
  - `post on instagram and facebook`
  - `post all`
  - `drop this on discord`
- `socio post chezahub` means the Boss has explicitly named both the brand and the Instagram+Facebook publish pair.
- `socio post playmechi` means the Boss has explicitly named both the brand and the Instagram+Facebook publish pair.
- `socio post mechi` or `post mechi` means `PlayMechi` and counts as approval to publish to Instagram + Facebook for PlayMechi.
- `socio instagram <brand>` means Instagram only for that brand.
- `socio facebook <brand>` means Facebook only for that brand.
- `socio x <brand>` means X only for that brand.
- TikTok direct and draft commands are paused. If the Boss asks for TikTok, say TikTok SMM automation is paused and ask whether to re-enable it.
- `socio instagram/facebook <brand>` means Instagram plus Facebook for that brand.
- `socio all <brand>` means Instagram, Facebook, and X for that brand unless the Boss explicitly includes TikTok.
- `socio ping` or `socio test` means return a short non-posting readiness reply for the current runtime.
- `socio help` means return the supported command list without publishing anything.
- `socio schedule ...` means create a scheduled post instead of publishing immediately. Do not ask for caption approval when brand, media, channels, and time are clear enough from the Boss's message.
- After publishing, always report the target channels, the returned post IDs or permalinks when available, and the exact caption used.
- After scheduling, always report the scheduled job id, brand, target channels, exact EAT time, and exact queued caption. Do not imply the post is already live.
- For explicit Boss post or schedule commands, execute first and notify after. Only ask a clarification when a required target or time is truly missing.
