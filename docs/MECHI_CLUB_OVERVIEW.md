## Tournament System

### Tournament Types

#### 1. Standard Tournaments (`/t/[slug]`)
- **Sizes**: 4, 8, or 16 players
- **Entry Fees**: Variable (free or paid)
- **Prize Pools**: Dynamic based on entries or fixed
- **Bracket System**: Single elimination
- **Statuses**: `open` → `full` → `active` → `completed`

#### 2. PlayMechi Weekend Cup (`/weekendcup`)
- **Format**: Multi-game weekend event
- **Season 1 Games**:
  - Friday: PUBG Mobile
  - Saturday: CODM
  - Sunday: eFootball + Free Fire
- **Entry Fees** (per game):
  - Early Bird: KSh 50
  - Regular: KSh 75
  - Late: KSh 100
- **Prize Pool**: Up to KSh 7,500 total
- **Features**:
  - Registration with payment
  - Check-in system
  - WhatsApp group per game
  - Live streaming

#### 3. Weka Mawe Weekly Challenge (`/playmechi/weka-mawe`)
- **Game**: eFootball (1v1)
- **Format**: 32-player single elimination bracket
- **Entry Fee**: KSh 100
- **Schedule**: Weekly (Saturdays)
- **Rounds**: Round of 32 → Round of 16 → Quarter-finals → Semi-finals → Final
- **Recording Required**: Quarter-finals onwards
- **Host**: @gamer_mastaa19

#### 4. Online Gaming Tournament (`/online-gaming-tournament`)
- **Games**: PUBG Mobile, CODM, eFootball
- **Format**: 3-night event
- **Streamed**: Live on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok

### Tournament Flow
1. **Registration** - Player signs up and pays entry fee
2. **Payment Confirmation** - Slot locked after payment
3. **Check-in** - Required on match day
4. **Bracket Generation** - Seeded by registration order
5. **Match Play** - Players compete and report scores
6. **Advancement** - Winners progress through bracket
7. **Prize Distribution** - Winner receives prize via M-Pesa

## Payment System

### Payment Provider
- **Paystack** - Primary payment processor
- **M-Pesa** - Mobile money integration

### Payment Flows

#### Tournament Entry
1. Player registers for tournament
2. Paystack checkout initiated
3. Payment confirmed via webhook
4. Slot locked and confirmed

#### Prize Disbursement
1. Tournament completes
2. Winner identified
3. M-Pesa disbursement initiated
4. Payout status tracked (`pending` → `paid` / `failed`)

### Payment Statuses
- `pending` - Awaiting payment
- `paid` - Payment confirmed
- `failed` - Payment failed
- `refunded` - Payment refunded
- `manual_review` - Requires admin review
